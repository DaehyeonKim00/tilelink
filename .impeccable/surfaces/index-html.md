# Surface brief: Tile Link catalogue home

Scope: the single-page catalogue site (all sections of one page). Visitor mode: Operate (find a product, then contact the business offline).

Audience, job and action: tilers, builders/interior firms and DIY visitors, many of them older, find a product by category, brand or search, then email, phone or visit. No online sale.

Content and proof: real product names, cut-out product photos in public/images, brand names; address and email are real, phone, opening hours and map are placeholders to be filled later; no prices, no popularity claims.

Constraints: Vite + React + TypeScript, frontend only, no backend or database. Base colour is the original navy #1a365d and blue #3182ce. Large, high-contrast type for older readers. 12 products per page. Empty categories hidden until they hold products. Admin mode kept, changes stored in the browser.

Unresolved: phone number, opening hours, map, featured product choice, brand logos where none can be found.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, readable trade catalogue where finding a product and reaching a person are always one step away; it refuses a shop-style interface with carts, prices and promotions.

OWN-WORLD: Original navy and blue on white with a faint blue construction grid as the page ground; solid white surfaces with 1px slate borders carry all text; one highly legible humanist sans at large sizes; plain rectangles with a small radius, no ornament, no tinted gradients.

STORY: The visitor sees at once that this is a tile and building-materials supplier, finds the product by category, brand or search, and knows how to call, email or visit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy header with logo, search and Call/Email buttons; a category bar below; left the headline "Professional Tile & Building Materials", one line of service promise and a large search field; right a single contact panel with email, phone and address rows; the featured products row starts at the fold; a Call button stays fixed at bottom right.

FORM: The category standard (canon), played straight at TradieCart-level craft; seed: bc977f17def1e227.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
